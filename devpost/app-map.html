Handful App Map

A reference map of the finished proof of concept. Snapshot of commit 582c919 plus the final wording edits. Code can drift from this page over time.

What the app is

Handful listens while a parent talks through a family recipe and turns rough amounts ("a handful of flour") into a recipe card a child can cook from. Mom's own words always stay next to the app's estimate. There are four main pieces:

  • Ears (in the browser): the built-in speech recognition, with a typing box as the last resort.
  • Helper (on the server): sends the words to Gemini, which only reads and sorts them. It never produces a converted amount.
  • Measuring table: the one fixed table where a rough word becomes a number.
  • Recipe box: finished recipes and their photos, kept in this browser only.

One action, from voice to card

A reference route (not toured live)

  1. Where the words are grouped. lib/understandingClient.ts, search createPhraseCollector. It waits for about a second of silence so an amount and its ingredient arrive together.
  2. Where the answer is checked and measured. app/api/understand/route.ts calls askGemini (lib/server/gemini.ts) and then checkAnswer (lib/server/checkAnswer.ts). In the browser, measureHeard in lib/measuringTable.ts turns "handful" into ¼ cup.
  3. Where it shows and is saved. app/session/page.tsx, search addHeard for the new line and saveRecipe for Finish. lib/recipeBox.ts writes to the browser's IndexedDB.

A practice to reuse

Let the AI read, and let a fixed table decide the numbers

Decision: Gemini only reports what mom said ("handful", how many); the app converts it. Evidence: devpost/spec.md (the fixed measuring table), lib/measuringTable.ts, and the checks in tests/measuringTable.test.ts, such as "two handfuls is half a cup". Because the number comes from a small table, it can be tested and changed without touching the AI. On another project, keep the model on interpretation and put anything that must be exact in plain, testable code.

Run it again

  • From the product folder: npm install, then npm run dev, then open http://localhost:3000. It needs GEMINI_API_KEY in .env.local (see .env.example).
  • Checks: npm test.
